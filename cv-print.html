Profile

PhD in Education (University of Glasgow, 2024) on how music teachers perceive and mediate creativity. Qualitative researcher in music education and creativity, with expertise in interviews, video-recorded observation and thematic analysis. Guest lecturer in qualitative methods at the University of Liverpool. Current work applies the same approach to how learners and teachers use AI tools in music education.

Research Focus

  • Creativity in music education — core PhD strand: developed an original theoretical framework and model of creativity, informed by Csikszentmihalyi's Domain-Field-Individual model (the Field as institutional gatekeeper selecting which novelty is transmitted) among other theories. Explains how teachers mediate three curriculum-defined creativity dimensions: sound exploration, improvisation, and composition. Related peer-reviewed journal article forthcoming (Music Education Research).

Independent project

  • Syn-Aesthetica (2025–present). Self-initiated and unfunded, with jewellery-design PhD collaborator Dong Ding, Edinburgh College of Art. Prototype-stage Python/C++ tool designed to combine librosa feature extraction with a self-trained lightweight emotion classifier (public affective-music datasets) to drive physical, kinetic output via Ding's sculptural mechanisms. My role: analysis pipeline, classifier training and the educational-evaluation framework.

Education

01/2019–06/2024 PhD in Education — University of Glasgow

Thesis: Music teachers' perceptions of creativity in the context of twenty-first century Chinese education (viva 27 September 2023). Supervisors: Prof. Oscar Odena, Prof. Kay Livingston.

Qualitative design: 17 semi-structured interviews, 24 lesson observations, critical-incident charting, researcher diary; thematic analysis in NVivo. Produced an original seven-stage model of creativity development in music education. Key finding: conservatoire-trained instrumentalists received formal improvisation training; generalist teachers engaged with it only informally.
